Reminders from the start or the end

Questions

How do I track pumping sessions?

Tap Start pumping when you sit down. The timer starts right away and keeps the right time even if you lock the phone or switch apps. Tap the left and right sides of the screen to add half an ounce (or 10 mL) each, then tap Done. The session goes into today's total, and the next-pump countdown starts. If you forgot to log one, add a past session with its start time and length.

How long does breast milk keep in the fridge, freezer and deep freezer?

The CDC's general guidance for freshly pumped milk is up to 4 hours at room temperature, up to 4 days in the fridge, and in the freezer within 6 months is best and up to 12 months is acceptable. The CDC gives one set of freezer times; Pumping Tracker starts a regular freezer at 6 months and a deep freezer at 12 months, and you can change all three. Thawed milk is good for up to 1 day in the fridge and should not be refrozen. This is general guidance, not medical advice.

What is a milk stash and how do I rotate it oldest first?

A milk stash is the pumped milk you keep in the fridge and freezer for later, often for going back to work, daycare or a NICU stay. Rotating it oldest first means using the bag that will expire soonest before the newer ones, so less milk goes to waste. In Pumping Tracker you add each bag with its amount, pumped date and where it is stored, and the app lines them up by use-by date and marks the one to use next.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. Everything you log is saved on your iPhone, so the timer, the reminders, the stash and the trends all work without a connection. Reminders are local notifications, scheduled on the phone.

Yes. There is no account to make. Sessions, bags and settings stay on your iPhone and are not sent to us or anyone else. The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. If you buy Pro, Apple handles the payment and RevenueCat confirms it with an anonymous id. The app never asks for your baby's name, birth date or photos. The privacy policy has the details.

Why count the next pump from the end of a session?

Many pumping apps count the next reminder from when a session started. On a day when a session runs long, that cuts into your break. Counting from the end keeps the full gap you planned. Pumping Tracker lets you pick either one.

Is this medical advice?

No. Pumping Tracker is a personal log. Storage times are general guidance with a link to the CDC; follow your care team's advice for your baby.
