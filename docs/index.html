Built for real pumping days

Exclusive pumping, a NICU stay, or getting ready to go back to work: the hardest part is keeping it all in your head at 3am. Pumping Tracker keeps count so you don't have to. Tap Start pumping, tap the plus buttons for each side, and you're done. Night mode is true black with soft warm text, so it is easy on your eyes in a dark room.

Your freezer stash, in order

Every bag gets a number, the day it was pumped, the amount and a use-by date from storage times you can edit (they start at 4 days in the fridge, 6 months in the freezer and 12 months in a deep freezer). A label card shows exactly what to write on the bag. Moving a bag to the fridge to thaw updates its use-by date.

Share with your care team or caregiver

With Pumping Pro, make a supply log PDF for your lactation consultant or pediatrician, or a caregiver sheet for daycare, the NICU or a sitter, with bags in use-next order and simple thawing notes.

Questions

Can the reminder count from the end of my session?

Yes. You choose from the start or from the end, and the interval. Change it any time in Settings.

Does the timer keep running if I leave the app?

Yes. The start time is saved the moment you tap Start pumping.

Ounces or milliliters?

Either. Switch in Settings and everything converts.

Is this medical advice?

No. Pumping Tracker is a personal log. Storage times are general guidance with a link to the CDC; follow your care team's advice for your baby.
