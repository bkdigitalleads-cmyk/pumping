Pumping Tracker

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Pumping Tracker ("the app") is a pumping log for iPhone, made by Brian Kershner. It is built so that what you log stays on your device.

What the app stores

Your sessions (times, lengths, left and right amounts, notes), your milk bags (numbers, dates, amounts, where they are stored) and your settings are saved only in the app's database on your iPhone. They are not uploaded, synced or sent to us or anyone else. The app has no account system and works offline.

The app never asks for your baby's name, birth date or photos.

Purchases

If you buy Pumping Pro, the purchase is processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat, a purchase-management service, to confirm purchases. RevenueCat receives a random anonymous identifier and your purchase history for this app, never your name, contacts or anything you logged. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules local reminders on your iPhone: the next-pump reminder, and one reminder before a free trial ends. They are created on the device. No push service is used and nothing is sent from a server.

Analytics and tracking

The app contains no analytics, no ads and no tracking. We do not collect usage data.

Exports

PDF and CSV files are created on your iPhone and go only where you choose to send them (print, email, AirDrop, Files and so on).

Deleting your data

Delete any session or bag in the app, use Settings, Delete all sessions and bags, or delete the app. Everything it stored goes with it.
