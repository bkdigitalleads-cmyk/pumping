Pumping Tracker is a pumping log for iPhone: a session timer with left and right amounts, today's total against your goal, next-pump reminders, a freezer stash in use-by order, trends and PDFs.

How do I log a session?

On Today, tap Start pumping. The timer starts right away. Tap the plus and minus buttons for each side (or tap the number to type it), then Finish and save. You can hide the session and come back; the timer keeps going. Missed one? Tap Log a past session.

How does the next-pump reminder work?

In Settings, turn on Remind me, choose From the start or From the end, and set the interval. With From the start, a 9:00 session and a 3 hour interval means a reminder at 12:00. With From the end, a session that ends at 9:20 means a reminder at 12:20. The reminder moves each time you log a session. If you don't get it, check that notifications for Pumping are on in iOS Settings, and that a Focus mode is not holding them.

How do I add a bag to my stash?

Open Stash and tap Add a bag. Set the amount, where it goes and the day you pumped it. The app shows a label card with the bag number, date, amount and use-by date to write on the bag. Tap a bag to mark it used, move it, or discard it. Moving a frozen bag to the fridge marks it as thawing, with a use-by of the next day.

Where do the use-by dates come from?

From the storage times in Settings, which start at 4 days in the fridge, 6 months in the freezer and 12 months in a deep freezer, based on the CDC's general guidance. You can change them. They are not medical advice; follow your care team's advice for your baby.

How do I share my supply log?

Open Trends. With Pumping Pro, choose a range and tap Create PDF. Free users can preview the log; the preview is marked SAMPLE. The caregiver sheet is on the Stash tab.

How do I restore a purchase?

Settings, Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account you bought Pumping Pro with.
